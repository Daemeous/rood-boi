# Fog flow: a GPU fog simulation fed by emitters

Status: design, 2026-10-17. Not started; waiting for the user's go-ahead.
Written from a read of the code; the cost figures are estimates to measure in
phase 8.

## What exists

- `pc/src/fog_sim.{h,cpp}`: the interactive ground fog.
  - **The field:** a 2D top-down field on the CPU, 64x64 cells per volume,
    up to 4 volumes. Each cell holds a density multiplier (1 at rest, at most
    1.5) and a velocity: semi-Lagrangian advection, vorticity, a Jacobi
    pressure solve.
  - **What pushes it:** actors, the ghost, and attack and spell stamps, each
    with a `fog_disturb_*` weight.
  - **Timing and state:** it steps once per vsync from `pc_fog_vsync`, is
    saved in save states (`CHUNK_FOG`), and reaches the shaders through
    medium texture slices 65..68.
  - **Limits:** it can't create fog or move it vertically.
- The fog volumes' renderer: `vk_fogvol.inc`, `fog_volume.glsl`,
  `fogvol_{light,light_rt,march,temporal}.comp`.
  - Up to 8 volumes, each with a 32^3 light grid.
  - A half-resolution march and a temporal pass.
  - Confine to room.
  - OpenGL marches the same `fv_march` per pixel in the present.
  - About 0.5-0.7 ms at internal scale 4.
- The haze froxels: history 96%, so they would smear fast smoke.
- Particles: emitter assets in `pc/assets/emitters`, the lighting item
  `emitter`, Library entries, and the schema kind `emitter`.
  - `render` must be `pixel`.
  - `Particles.md` reserves "fog emitters".
- OpenGL tries a 4.6 core context first; `pc_gl_compute` gates compute.

## Naming

**Fog flow**, so it doesn't clash with `fog_sim`, which stays as is:
- files `fog_flow.*` and shaders `fogflow_*.comp`;
- the setting `fog_flow`, the room settings `fog_flow_*`, the RPC `fog_flow`;
- emitters whose asset has `render: "fog"`.

## 1. What "sim" means, and the choice

| Option | Gives | Misses | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Animated noise: analytic plumes plus capsule carving | Stateless, deterministic, OpenGL 3.3 | Accumulation, pooling, flow around walls, wakes, mixing | ~0 sim, about +0.1 ms march |
| B. Extend the 2D sim | Mist pouring across the floor, door drafts | Rising columns (braziers, steam) | CPU |
| C. A low-res 3D GPU grid per room | All of it: rising smoke, sinking mist, pooling, flow around pillars, wakes, wind | Needs readback for save states; GPU time | 0.1-0.3 ms sim plus the march |

**Recommendation: C as the main path, with A as the Low rung and the fallback
without compute.** Both are driven by the same emitter assets.

**Domain.** The room's interior: the floor cells' extent, padded by one cell.
In y it runs from the lowest floor + `fog_flow_under` to the highest floor −
`fog_flow_height`. Cells are cubic, and at least `fog_flow_cell` (12+) units.

**State** (3D images):

| Image | Format | Holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| density | RGBA16F x2 | rgb = albedo x density, a = extinction |
| velocity | RGBA16F x2 | xyz velocity, w = temperature |
| pressure | R32F x2 | |
| divergence | R32F | |
| curl | RGBA16F | scratch |
| solid | R8UI | |

**Step.** Every pass is a gather (no float atomics), so the result is
bit-deterministic on a given device and driver.
1. **Inject:**
   - up to 16 fog emitters, with a seeded pulse;
   - up to 18 bodies: capsules from `pc_dev_actors`, giving a moving
     boundary inside each and an outward push around it;
   - up to 8 live stamps.
2. **Advect velocity:** manual trilinear; MacCormack at Ultra.
3. **Curl and forces:**
   - buoyancy from temperature: hot rises, cold mist sinks;
   - vorticity confinement;
   - wind: the room's `env fog wind` x `fog_flow_wind`, plus deterministic
     gusts;
   - cooling.
4. **Projection:** divergence, then Jacobi (8 / 16 / 24 iterations,
   warm-started), then project. Solid cells are free-slip.
5. **Advect scalars:** with dissipation, and a sponge at the open borders.
6. **Reduce:** the active AABB, mass and a checksum, all with integer
   atomics, into an SSBO the march reads directly.

**Solid mask:**
- phase 2: from the floor grid (below the floor, or over `PC_FLOOR_EMPTY`);
- phase 7: plus voxelized wall and ceiling triangles.

Rendering keeps `medium_confine` and the void stop.

**Stepping.**
- `pc_fog_vsync` appends a per-vsync source record (bodies, stamps, time,
  emitters) to a ring of 64.
- The renderer runs every owed step in order, so the result doesn't depend on
  frame rate.
- An overflow counts as `dropped` and the sim restarts.
- A room change or a jump over 10 s restarts it.

**Save states.** A new snapshot chunk `CHUNK_FOG_FLOW` holds the clock, the
ring and the images. They are read back on save and uploaded on load, in a
renderer-neutral format.

## 2. Renderers

**Vulkan.**
- New `vk_fogflow.inc`: one compute pipeline per pass, sharing
  `fogflow_sim.glsl`.
- **Drawn through the fog volumes' renderer, not the froxels.** When the
  domain has mass, it takes fogvol slot 0 as a box volume with a new flag
  (`fill.w + 32`).
- `fv_density` reads the flow texture (density, albedo; detail flow-mapped
  by its velocity).
- `fv_hit` clips to the active AABB.
- Its lights are chosen by distance to the active AABB's centre.
- Haze froxels are unchanged.

**OpenGL with compute.**
- New `fog_flow_gl.c` compiles the same `fogflow_sim.glsl` (add it to
  `gl_shaders.py` FRAGMENTS).
- `gl_present.c` binds density and velocity on units 21 and 22, with a
  `PC_NO_FOG_FLOW` fallback.

**Without compute:** the analytic rung only.

**The CPU reference** (`fog_flow_ref.cpp`) is for tests, not a runtime path.

**The analytic rung (Low).**
- Each fog emitter becomes a "plume" volume (flag + 64): an axis along its
  exit velocity bent by wind and heat, a widening radius, falling density,
  and noise puffs scrolled along the axis.
- Actors carve capsules analytically.

## 3. Data model

**Emitter asset section** (`"render": {"type": "fog"}`, `"quality": "fog"`):
```json
"fog": { "rate": 0.006, "velocity": [0, -60, 0], "spread": 0.25, "heat": 1.0,
         "force": 0, "color": [0.8, 0.8, 0.82], "fill": [0, 0, 0], "glow": [0, 0, 0],
         "pulse": 0.4, "pulse_speed": 0.6 }
```
- `heat < 0` is cold, sinking mist.
- `force` with `rate` 0 makes a draft.
- Assets to ship: `brazier_smoke`, `candle_smoke`, `vent_steam`, `grate_mist`,
  `drain_steam`, `door_draft`.

**Lighting-layer fields**, appended after `quality`: `fog_rate`,
`fog_velocity`, `fog_spread`, `fog_heat`, `fog_force`, `fog_color`, `fog_fill`,
`fog_glow`, `fog_pulse`, `fog_pulse_speed`. Mirror them in
`lighting_format.py`, `lighting_lint.py` and `lighting/README.md`.

**Room settings** (Atmosphere page):

| Group | Settings |
| --- | --- |
| Behaviour | `fog_flow_density`, `_dissipate`, `_cool`, `_buoyancy`, `_swirl` |
| Wind | `fog_flow_wind`, `_wind_rate`, `_gust`, `_gust_period` |
| Actors | `fog_flow_push`, `_push_radius` |
| Domain | `fog_flow_height`, `_under`, `_cell`, `_walls` |
| Look | `fog_flow_opacity`, `_brightness`, `_phase`, `_ambient`, `_shadow`, `_detail` |

**The graphics setting `fog_flow`:** 0 Off, 1 Low (analytic), 2 Medium,
3 High, 4 Ultra.
- It follows the lighting presets.
- It is clamped to Low without compute.
- `fog_flow_max_cells` and `fog_flow_jacobi` override the defaults.

**Editor:**
- **Library:** fog emitters are dropped from the Library. On a wall, they
  face out along the surface normal (`add_on_surface` gains the normal).
- **Details:** a `fog_emitter` schema kind with the categories Basics,
  Source, Flow, Look and Transform.
- **Extras:** Reset, Freeze, Step, and debug views (density slice, velocity
  arrows, solid mask).
- **Thumbnail:** an analytic plume.

**RPC `fog_flow`:**
- Parameters: `level`, `settings`, `reset`, `freeze`, `step n`, `readback`,
  `sample [[x,y,z]...]`, `reference {steps}`, `debug`.
- The reply reports `path`, `dims`, `cell`, `steps`, `dropped`, `active_lo`
  / `active_hi`, `mass`, `checksum` and the timings.

## 4. Performance (scale 4, 1440x1080, estimates)

| Level | Grid | Rate | Jacobi | Sim | Render increment | Memory |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Off | — | — | — | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Low | analytic | — | — | 0 | ≤ 0.10 ms | 0 |
| Medium | 48x24x48 | 30 Hz | 8 | ≤ 0.06 ms | ≤ 0.20 ms | ~3 MB |
| High | 64x32x64 | 60 Hz | 16 | ≤ 0.15 ms | ≤ 0.30 ms | ~8 MB |
| Ultra | 96x40x96 | 60 Hz | 24 | ≤ 0.35 ms | ≤ 0.40 ms | ~21 MB |

OpenGL marches per pixel, so it defaults to Medium at most.

## 5. Tests

**`fog_flow_probe`** (CTest, the CPU reference on a 16x8x16 grid) checks:
- divergence below 1e-4 after projection;
- solid cells stay clean, with no leaks into empty columns;
- a hot source rises; cold mist spreads along the floor;
- wind moves the plume downwind;
- a moving capsule clears its inside and leaves a wake;
- mass halves at the dissipation rate;
- the same seed gives bit-identical state;
- the asset and lighting fields round-trip.

**`fog_flow_smoke.py`** (both renderers, headless RPC):
- **Mass:** it settles to equilibrium, with the centroid above a brazier.
- **Confinement:** samples over the void and under the floor read zero.
- **Actors:** Ashley clears the plume where she stands.
- **Controls:** reset and freeze work.
- **Determinism:** a save state followed by the same run gives a bitwise
  identical checksum.
- **Agreement:** the GPU matches the CPU reference within half-float error;
  Vulkan and OpenGL agree on mass and centroid within 5%.
- **Off:** pixel-identical to no emitter.
- **Picture and Low:** the plume brightens its screen box; the analytic path
  draws without compute.
- **Opt-in perf** (`--perf`): the GPU cost.
- **Editor:** a Library drop on a wall, the Details kind, undo.

## 6. Phases (one agent each; worktree, `$ROOD_HEAVY_LOCK`, no `src/` edits)

1. **Plan doc and CPU reference:** `fog_flow_ref.{h,cpp}`, `fog_flow_probe`.
2. **Data model:**
   - lighting fields, the asset `fog` section, particles skipping fog
     emitters;
   - the shipped assets;
   - lint and format tooling;
   - the `fog_flow` setting and the room settings.
3. **Host side:** `fog_flow.{h,cpp}` (domain, solid mask, source ring, emitter
   packing); the `fog_sim` body and event accessors; the RPC skeleton.
4. **Vulkan sim:** shaders, `vk_fogflow.inc`, readback, perf section; RPC
   checks.
5. **Vulkan rendering:** the flow domain in the fog volumes, `fv_density` and
   `fv_hit`, bindings, debug views, picture tests.
6. **OpenGL:** `fog_flow_gl.c`, `gl_present.c` units and fallback,
   cross-renderer checks.
7. **Analytic rung, walls and save states:** the plume shape, wall
   voxelization, the snapshot chunk; Low-path and save/load determinism
   tests.
8. **Editor and docs:** the `fog_emitter` kind, Library placement by normal,
   Details extras, Atmosphere page, thumbnail; `Fog_Flow.md`, `Particles.md`,
   `Vulkan_Renderer.md`; measured costs.

**Risks:**
- light selection for a room-sized volume;
- the fog volume history's disocclusion with fast smoke (may need a lower
  history for the domain);
- OpenGL texture-unit pressure;
- domain resizes restart the sim.
